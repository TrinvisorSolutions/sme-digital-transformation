# Business Requirements

## Purpose

The purpose of this document is to translate the problems identified during discovery into clear business requirements.

Business requirements describe **what the organisation needs to achieve**.

They do not yet specify the exact technology or product that should be used.

## Business Requirements

### BR-01: Centralised Customer Information

The organisation requires a single, reliable source of customer information that can be accessed by authorised teams.

**Business rationale**

Customer information is currently fragmented across spreadsheets, emails and separate systems, leading to duplication and inconsistent records.

**Expected benefit**

* Improved data accuracy
* Reduced duplicate records
* Faster access to customer information
* Improved customer service

---

### BR-02: Standardised Customer Onboarding

The organisation requires a consistent customer onboarding process across relevant departments.

**Business rationale**

Current onboarding practices vary between employees and teams.

**Expected benefit**

* Improved consistency
* Clear ownership
* Faster onboarding
* Reduced risk of missed information
* Improved customer experience

---

### BR-03: Reduced Manual Data Entry

The organisation requires a reduction in repeated manual entry of the same information across multiple systems.

**Business rationale**

Employees currently enter customer information several times during the onboarding and service process.

**Expected benefit**

* Reduced administrative workload
* Lower risk of human error
* Improved employee productivity
* Faster processing

---

### BR-04: Improved Workflow Visibility

The organisation requires visibility of where customers, enquiries and key activities are within business processes.

**Business rationale**

Current processes rely heavily on email and spreadsheets, making it difficult to identify ownership, delays and outstanding actions.

**Expected benefit**

* Improved accountability
* Faster resolution of delays
* Better operational oversight
* Improved workload management

---

### BR-05: Reliable Management Reporting

Management requires timely and consistent access to operational, customer and sales performance information.

**Business rationale**

Current reporting requires significant manual reconciliation and may rely on inconsistent data sources.

**Expected benefit**

* Faster reporting
* Improved decision-making
* Increased confidence in management information
* Earlier identification of performance issues

---

### BR-06: Consistent KPI Definitions

The organisation requires agreed definitions for key performance indicators used across departments.

**Business rationale**

Different teams may interpret or calculate the same performance measure differently.

**Expected benefit**

* Consistent reporting
* Improved accountability
* Better comparison across teams
* Increased trust in management information

---

### BR-07: Data Quality Controls

The organisation requires controls to improve the completeness, accuracy and consistency of business-critical data.

**Business rationale**

Current processes provide limited validation when information is entered or updated.

**Expected benefit**

* Reduced data errors
* Improved reporting quality
* Reduced reconciliation effort
* Improved regulatory and operational control

---

### BR-08: Appropriate Workflow Automation

The organisation requires automation of suitable repetitive and rules-based activities.

**Business rationale**

Employees currently spend significant time on manual administration and coordination.

**Expected benefit**

* Reduced administrative effort
* Faster processing
* Greater consistency
* Improved scalability

Automation should only be introduced after the relevant process has been reviewed and standardised.

---

### BR-09: Role-Based Access

The organisation requires appropriate controls over who can view, create, update and manage business information.

**Business rationale**

A centralised system must protect sensitive information while allowing employees to access the information required for their roles.

**Expected benefit**

* Improved security
* Better information governance
* Reduced risk of unauthorised access
* Clear accountability

---

### BR-10: Scalable Operating Model

The organisation requires business processes and systems that can support continued growth without a proportional increase in administrative effort.

**Business rationale**

Current manual processes may become increasingly difficult and costly to manage as the organisation grows.

**Expected benefit**

* Improved scalability
* Lower operational complexity
* Better resource utilisation
* Reduced dependence on manual work

## Requirement Priorities

An initial MoSCoW prioritisation is proposed.

### Must Have

* BR-01 Centralised Customer Information
* BR-02 Standardised Customer Onboarding
* BR-04 Improved Workflow Visibility
* BR-05 Reliable Management Reporting
* BR-07 Data Quality Controls
* BR-09 Role-Based Access

### Should Have

* BR-03 Reduced Manual Data Entry
* BR-06 Consistent KPI Definitions
* BR-08 Appropriate Workflow Automation

### Could Have

Additional enhancements may be identified during solution design, including advanced analytics, AI-assisted workflows and self-service reporting.

### Won't Have Yet

Highly complex automation or advanced AI capability should not be prioritised in the first phase unless a clear business case, adequate data quality and appropriate controls exist.

## Requirements Traceability

Each business requirement should later be linked to:

* Functional requirements
* Process changes
* Solution components
* Test scenarios
* Business benefits

This will make it possible to demonstrate that the final solution addresses the original business problems rather than introducing technology without a clear purpose.
