# Stakeholder Analysis

## Purpose

The purpose of this stakeholder analysis is to identify the key groups affected by the digital transformation initiative, understand their level of influence and interest, and determine how they should be engaged throughout the project.

## Power and Interest Assessment

### High Power, High Interest

These stakeholders should be managed closely:

* Managing Director
* Operations Director
* Head of Sales
* Finance Manager
* IT Lead

They have significant influence over the project and are directly affected by its success.

### High Power, Lower Day-to-Day Interest

These stakeholders require concise, decision-focused communication:

* Senior Management Team

They may not need to participate in every workshop, but they require visibility of progress, risks, costs and expected benefits.

### Medium Power, High Interest

These stakeholders should be actively involved in discovery, design and testing:

* Sales Team
* Customer Service Team
* Finance Team

Their day-to-day experience is essential for identifying practical issues and ensuring that proposed solutions are usable.

### Lower Direct Power, High Impact

* Customers

Customers may not make internal project decisions, but the transformation should improve their experience. Their needs should therefore be considered through customer journey analysis and service performance measures.

## Potential Stakeholder Concerns

Key concerns may include:

* Resistance to changing established working practices
* Concern that automation could affect existing roles
* Lack of confidence in new systems
* Increased workload during implementation
* Concerns around data migration and accuracy
* Different departments prioritising different outcomes
* Technology solutions being selected before business requirements are fully understood
* Insufficient training or support

## Engagement Principles

Stakeholder engagement should be:

* Early
* Transparent
* Role-specific
* Evidence-based
* Focused on business outcomes
* Continuous throughout the project

The transformation should not be designed only by senior management or technology teams. End users should be involved throughout discovery, design, testing and implementation.
