# Problem Statement

## Current Situation

The organisation has grown rapidly, but its internal processes, systems and reporting practices have not developed at the same pace.

Teams across Sales, Customer Service, Operations and Finance rely on a combination of spreadsheets, email and disconnected tools to manage customer information, operational activity and reporting.

As a result, employees frequently spend time manually transferring information between systems, checking data accuracy and reconciling conflicting records.

## Business Problem

The absence of standardised processes and integrated systems is creating operational inefficiency, data inconsistency and limited visibility for management.

Customer information is fragmented across multiple sources, which increases the risk of duplicate records, incomplete information and inconsistent customer experiences.

Manual reporting also makes it difficult for managers to access timely and reliable information when making operational and strategic decisions.

## Key Symptoms

The organisation is experiencing:

* Duplicate and inconsistent customer records
* Multiple versions of spreadsheets containing similar information
* Manual data entry across departments
* Slow customer onboarding
* Repetitive administrative tasks
* Heavy dependence on email for workflow coordination
* Limited visibility of customer and operational performance
* Time-consuming monthly reporting
* Inconsistent processes across teams
* Increased risk of human error

## Business Impact

These issues affect both operational efficiency and the organisation's ability to scale.

Employees spend time completing administrative work that could potentially be automated or simplified.

Management lacks a consistent view of performance, making it harder to identify problems, monitor trends and make timely decisions.

Fragmented customer information may also affect service quality, response times and the overall customer experience.

If these issues continue as the organisation grows, operational complexity and cost are likely to increase.

## Desired Future State

The organisation requires a more integrated and standardised operating environment where:

* Customer information is managed consistently
* Key processes are clearly defined
* Manual activities are reduced where appropriate
* Information moves efficiently between teams
* Management can access reliable performance information
* Reporting is faster and more consistent
* Employees have clear responsibilities and workflows
* Technology supports business processes rather than creating additional administrative work

The transformation should improve efficiency while supporting future growth without introducing unnecessary technical complexity.
